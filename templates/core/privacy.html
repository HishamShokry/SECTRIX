How we handle your information

Last updated {{ last_updated }}.

What we collect

This site collects personal data in one place only: the contact form. When you submit it we store the name, work email address, phone number, company, job title, country and organization size you provide, together with the message you write, the time of submission, your browser's user-agent string, and a keyed one-way hash of your IP address.

We store a hash rather than the address itself so the value cannot be read back as an IP. It is still linkable to you, so we treat it as personal data and it is covered by everything below. Our web server additionally records access logs in which client IP addresses are truncated before they are written.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and the site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or stylesheets. The office map on our contact page is embedded from OpenStreetMap, which will see your IP address if that section loads.

Why we hold it, and on what basis

We use what you send solely to respond to your enquiry and to carry out any engagement that follows from it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to a business approach you initiated, and — where an engagement follows — performance of a contract. We do not use your details for marketing unrelated to your enquiry, and we do not sell, rent or share them with third parties.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are retained for {{ retention_days }} days after they are closed, then deleted. If an enquiry becomes a client engagement, the associated records are governed by that engagement's contract instead.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to our holding it at all. Write to {{ site.email }} and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over TLS, enquiry data is held in an access-controlled database, and administrative access requires an individual account. If you believe you have found a vulnerability in this site, please report it to {{ site.email }}; we will acknowledge your report and will not pursue action against good-faith research.
